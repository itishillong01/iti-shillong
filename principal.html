Principal's Message

Shri. Bonison Sawkmie

Principal, Industrial Training Institute Shillong

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Industrial Training Institute Shillong. Our institute is committed to providing quality vocational training that combines technical knowledge with practical skills, discipline, and professionalism. We encourage our trainees to make the most of their time at ITI Shillong, develop confidence in their abilities, and prepare themselves for meaningful careers and future opportunities. With dedication, hard work, and the right skills, every trainee can contribute positively to society and build a successful future. I wish all our trainees the very best in their studies and future endeavours.

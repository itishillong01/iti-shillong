Industrial Training Institute Shillong

Industrial Training Institute (ITI) Shillong is a vocational training institution dedicated to providing quality technical and skill-based education to young people. The institute focuses on developing practical skills, technical knowledge, and industry-oriented competencies that help trainees prepare for employment, self-employment, and further career opportunities.
